About the Property

Your Home in Cavtat

Apartments Joy is a family-run property in Cavtat — one of the Adriatic's most charming towns, just 20 minutes from Dubrovnik.

The property offers three individually furnished apartments, each with its own character. Whether you're waking up to a sea view in Alpha, enjoying the modern comforts of Ana, or relaxing on the sun-drenched terrace, every stay feels like home.

A shared outdoor area with seating, a gazebo, and a swing chair is the perfect spot for long evenings under the stars.
