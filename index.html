About the Villa

A Home Kissed by the Sun

Villa Joy is a beautifully restored Mediterranean villa where every detail whispers relaxation, warmth, and timeless elegance.

Spread over 350 m² across two levels, the villa embraces light and breeze at every turn. Stone arches, terracotta floors, and hand-painted tiles connect tradition with contemporary comfort — while panoramic sea views remind you why you came.

The private garden, shaded pergola, and 12-metre infinity pool make outdoor living as irresistible as the cool, airy interiors. Whether you're gathering for a long lunch or watching the stars from the terrace, Villa Joy sets the scene.
